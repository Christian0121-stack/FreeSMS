About FreeSMSPH

Connecting Filipino hearts worldwide through free, reliable SMS services

Our Story

FreeSMSPH was born from a simple belief: communication should be accessible to everyone. Founded in 2020 by a team of passionate Filipino technologists, we saw how expensive SMS rates were keeping families and friends apart.

As OFWs ourselves, we understood the pain of wanting to stay connected but being limited by costly messaging services. That's when we decided to create a solution - a completely free SMS service that would help Filipinos stay in touch without breaking the bank.

Today, we're proud to serve over 50,000 users across the Philippines and beyond, facilitating millions of messages that keep relationships strong and businesses running.

Our Mission

Eliminating communication barriers for all Filipinos

Our Mission

To provide free, reliable, and secure communication services that keep Filipino families and communities connected, regardless of their financial situation or geographic location.

We envision a Philippines where distance and cost never stand in the way of meaningful connections. Every message sent through our platform is a step toward a more connected and unified Filipino community.

Beyond just SMS, we're building a comprehensive communication ecosystem that includes voice services, business solutions, and developer tools - all with the same commitment to accessibility and affordability.
