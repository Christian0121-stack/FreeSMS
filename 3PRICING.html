Frequently Asked Questions

Talaga bang free ang SMS?
Oo! Ang Free Plan ay walang bayad forever. Pwede kang mag-send ng 5 SMS sa lahat ng networks gamit ang free account mo sa Pilipinas nang libre.
Can I upgrade or downgrade anytime?
Yes! You can upgrade to Pro or Enterprise anytime. Downgrading is also flexible - no long-term contracts required.
How fast are the SMS deliveries?
Our SMS are typically delivered within 5-30 seconds. Pro and Enterprise users get priority routing for even faster delivery.
May additional charges ba for other networks?
Wala! All networks sa Pilipinas (Globe, Smart, Sun, TNT, TM, etc.) ay covered na sa lahat ng plans namin.
Is there a mobile app?
Yes! Pro and Enterprise users get access to our mobile apps for iOS and Android. Free users can use our responsive web interface.
How secure is the platform?
Very secure! We use enterprise-grade encryption, secure servers, and never store your messages longer than necessary.
