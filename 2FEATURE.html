Who We Are

FreeSMSPH is the Philippines' leading free SMS service provider, dedicated to keeping Filipinos connected without the burden of high messaging costs.

Founded by a team of passionate Filipino developers and telecommunications experts, we understand the unique communication needs of our kababayans both locally and abroad.

Our mission is to make communication accessible to everyone, regardless of their financial situation or location. We believe that staying connected with family and friends should never be a luxury.

What We Do

We provide complete SMS services to help Filipinos stay connected with their loved ones. Our platform supports all major networks in the Philippines including Globe, Smart, Sun, TNT, and TM.

Beyond personal messaging, we also offer business solutions including bulk SMS services, API integrations for developers, and enterprise communication tools for organizations.

Our advanced technology ensures fast, reliable message delivery while maintaining the highest standards of security and privacy for all our users.
