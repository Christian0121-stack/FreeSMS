Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about FreeSMSPH services

🔍
FreeSMSPH is the Philippines' leading free SMS service provider that allows you to send text messages to all Philippine networks without any cost. We offer a user-friendly platform for personal and business communication needs.
New users get 5 free SMS messages upon registration. After that, you can purchase additional messages through our affordable pricing plans or earn free credits through our referral program.
FreeSMSPH supports all major Philippine networks including Globe, Smart, Sun, TNT, TM, and other local carriers. Messages are delivered instantly across all supported networks.
Creating an account is simple! Click on "Sign Up" in the top navigation, fill out the registration form with your details, verify your email address, and you're ready to start sending free SMS messages.
Yes, you can update your account information anytime by logging in and going to the Profile section in your user menu. You can change your name, contact information, and password from there.
After your free messages, SMS costs vary by package. Check our Pricing page for detailed information. We offer competitive rates starting from as low as ₱0.50 per message with bulk discounts available.
We accept various payment methods including GCash, PayMaya, bank transfers, credit/debit cards, and over-the-counter payments at 7-Eleven, Bayad Center, and other partner locations.
SMS delivery issues can occur due to network congestion, invalid recipient numbers, or carrier-specific delays. Check that the recipient number is correct and active. If problems persist, contact our support team.
Standard SMS messages have a 160-character limit. Messages longer than 160 characters will be split into multiple SMS and charged accordingly. Our platform shows you the character count as you type.
Yes! Premium users can schedule SMS messages to be sent at a specific date and time. This feature is available in your dashboard after upgrading to a paid plan.
Absolutely! We use enterprise-grade security measures including SSL encryption, secure data storage, and strict privacy policies. Your personal information and messages are protected with the highest security standards.
If you wish to delete your account, please contact our support team. We'll guide you through the process and ensure all your data is properly removed from our systems according to our privacy policy.
