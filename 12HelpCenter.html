Quick Start Guide

1

Create Your Account

Sign up for free using your email address. Verify your email to activate your account and receive 5 free SMS messages.

2

Compose Your Message

Navigate to the messaging interface, enter the recipient's phone number, and type your message. Check the character count to stay within limits.

3

Send & Track

Hit send and track your message delivery status in real-time. View your message history and manage your contacts in your dashboard.

4

Add Credits

When you need more messages, easily add credits to your account using various payment methods available in the Philippines.
